US office-supplies retailer

Retail Sales Performance

Executive view of sales, profit and margin with year-over-year comparisons, the products and states that lose money, and how discounts erode margin.

Sample Superstore · 9,994 order lines · FY2014–FY2017

Key findings

  • FY2017 sales reached $733K, +20.4% vs. FY2016.
  • Margin is 29.5% on full-price sales but turns negative (−37.3%) above a 20% discount.
  • Tables, Bookcases and Supplies lose money; Tables alone loses $17.7K.
  • 10 of 49 states operate at a loss (combined −$98K), led by Texas, Ohio and Pennsylvania.

Report pages

  • Executive OverviewSales, profit, margin, orders, AOV and customers with YoY change; monthly trend vs. prior year; region, category and segment mix.
  • ProfitabilityProfit by sub-category with losses highlighted, discount depth vs. margin, products with the largest losses.
  • Customers & MarketsState scorecard, loss-making states, top customers and fulfillment time by ship mode.

How it's built

Star schema built in Power Query: Sales fact plus Product, Customer and a generated Date table marked for time intelligence. 40 DAX measures in display folders, including YoY comparisons, top/bottom-N rankings and dynamic narrative subtitles. Data prep fixed 5-digit postal codes, non-breaking spaces and 32 duplicate product IDs.
