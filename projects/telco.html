Telecom Customer Churn

Who is leaving, how much monthly revenue walks out with them, and which services and contract types keep customers.

IBM Telco Customer Churn · 7,043 customers

Key findings

  • 26.5% of customers churned, taking $139K of $456K in monthly recurring revenue (30.5%).
  • Month-to-month contracts churn at 42.7% vs. 2.8% on two-year contracts.
  • First-year customers churn at 47.4% vs. 9.5% after four years.
  • High-risk profile (month-to-month + fiber + electronic check): 18.6% of customers, 60.4% churn, 49% of lost revenue.

Report pages

  • Churn OverviewChurn rate, churned customers and lost monthly revenue; churn by tenure, contract, payment method, internet service and age group.
  • Services & PricingChurn with vs. without each service, churn by monthly bill and by number of protection add-ons.
  • Revenue at RiskLost revenue by contract and internet segment, the high-risk customer profile and a segment scorecard.

How it's built

Customers table plus a Services table unpivoted in Power Query (one row per customer and service) with a bidirectional relationship, so churn can be compared for subscribers and non-subscribers of every service on one chart. 38 DAX measures, with groups above the average churn highlighted automatically.
