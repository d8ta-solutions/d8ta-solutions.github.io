People analytics

HR Attrition Analytics

Why employees leave, which roles and pay bands are most at risk, and what turnover costs the business.

IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition · 1,470 employees

Key findings

  • Overall attrition is 16.1%; Sales Representatives reach 39.8% (2.5×).
  • Employees working overtime leave at 30.5%, 2.9× the rate of those who don't.
  • 43% of leavers had 2 years of tenure or less.
  • Leavers earned 30% less per month than employees who stayed; estimated replacement cost is $6.8M.

Report pages

  • Workforce OverviewHeadcount, leavers, attrition rate, age, pay and tenure; attrition by job role, age, department and marital status.
  • Attrition DriversOvertime, tenure, job satisfaction and business travel.
  • Compensation & CareerAttrition by pay band, pay of leavers vs. stayers by job level and a retention scorecard by role.

How it's built

Single employee table shaped in Power Query: constant columns removed, 1–4 survey scores mapped to labels, and age, tenure and income bands with sort orders. 39 DAX measures, including a selection-aware benchmark that drives the conditional colors and dynamic subtitles.
