Customer experience survey

Airline Passenger Experience

Which parts of the journey drive satisfaction, how it differs by cabin and trip type, and the cost of delays.

Airline satisfaction survey · 129,880 passengers · 14 touchpoints

Key findings

  • Overall satisfaction is 43.4%: 69.4% in Business vs. 18.8% in Economy.
  • Online boarding is the strongest driver: 87% of passengers who score it 5 are satisfied vs. 13% for scores 1–3.
  • Inflight Wi-Fi is the lowest-rated touchpoint (2.81 / 5).
  • On-time arrivals: 45.7% satisfied vs. 35.8% when late.

Report pages

  • Experience OverviewSatisfaction rate, service rating, loyalty, trip mix and punctuality; satisfaction by age, cabin, segment, customer type and distance.
  • Service QualityAverage rating per touchpoint, the top 7 drivers that separate satisfied from dissatisfied passengers, and satisfaction by online boarding score.
  • Operations & PunctualitySatisfaction by arrival delay and Wi-Fi score, plus a segment scorecard.

How it's built

Passengers table plus a Service Ratings table unpivoted in Power Query (one row per passenger and touchpoint), so every touchpoint is compared on one axis. Train/test files combined; ratings of 0 treated as not applicable. 36 DAX measures.
