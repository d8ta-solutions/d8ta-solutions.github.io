Restaurant market

Restaurant Ratings Analysis

What separates highly rated restaurants from the rest: review volume, price range and the listings nobody rates.

Zomato restaurant dataset · 9,551 restaurants

Key findings

  • More than 1 in 5 restaurants (2,148) have no rating at all.
  • Average rating climbs from 2.97 with fewer than 10 reviews to 4.20 with 1,000+.
  • Premium restaurants average 3.89 vs. 3.24 for budget ones.

Report pages

  • Executive SummaryRestaurants, average rating, share rated 4.0+, votes, cost for two and delivery coverage; rating by country and the distribution of ratings.
  • MarketsIndicators by country, price vs. quality positioning and the top-rated cities.
  • Cuisines & PriceMost offered and best-rated cuisines, the restaurants with most engagement, and services and quality by price level.

How it's built

Restaurant listings cleaned in Power Query with review-volume and price-range bands, analysed in a multi-page Power BI report.
